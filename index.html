BULBASAUR

#0001

Bulbasaur is one of the three Pokémon the player can choose from when they start "Pokémon Red" and "Pokémon Green," the first installments of the Pokémon game series and thus the beginning of generation 1.

Fun Fact: Bulbasaur is based on the real life horned frog.
